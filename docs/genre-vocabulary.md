# Genre vocabulary — R is canonical

Decision taken: **`D:\MP3\Resorted` (R) is the canonical vocabulary.** `D:\MP3\Tracks` (T) is the
back catalogue and contributes names only where they earn a place.

Why R wins, measured:

| | T | R |
|---|---:|---:|
| names | 118 | 91 |
| internal spelling forks | 4 | **1** |
| names absent from the other list | 69 | 42 |

13 of the 14 fork groups in the previous draft were T-vs-R collisions, not real ambiguity.
Declaring R canonical dissolves all 13 without a decision.
